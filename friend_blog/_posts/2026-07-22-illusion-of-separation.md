When I left home at 14, I quickly learned that the world is not as fragmented as it seems. We are all deeply intertwined with the environments we pass through and the people we meet.

The cosmic joke is that we spend our whole lives trying to find ourselves, only to realize we were never truly separate from the whole to begin with. The 'I' is just a temporary wave on the infinite ocean of the Tao.
