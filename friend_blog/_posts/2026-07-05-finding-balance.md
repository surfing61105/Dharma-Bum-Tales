Zen is not about escaping the world, but finding your center within the storm. As I traveled across the country, sleeping under the stars and hitchhiking through the rain, equanimity became my greatest tool.

It's easy to be peaceful on a mountaintop. It's much harder to maintain that peace when the world is demanding your reaction. The true practice happens in the messy, loud, and unpredictable moments of daily life.
